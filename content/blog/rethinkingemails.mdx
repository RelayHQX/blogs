Email hasn't become painful just because we receive too many messages. It has become painful because the way we interact with email hasn't evolved in thirty years.

Open an email. Read it. Hunt for the crucial two sentences. Scroll past a wall of signatures, legal disclaimers, and quoted threads. Find the reply button. Wait for a spinner. Repeat fifty times a day.

We believe it can be fundamentally simpler.

> **"We don't want you to manage your inbox. We want you to get things done and move on."**

We're building **Relay Mail** to bring the effortless simplicity of instant messaging into email — not by squeezing every message into an awkward chat bubble, but by making the entire experience feel natural, immediate, and distraction-free.

## 1. Email Should Feel Like a Conversation

We love how modern messaging apps work: you open a thread, instantly understand what's happening, reply, and move on with your day.

Why can't email feel that effortless?

With Relay Mail:

- <MessageSquare className="size-4 inline text-emerald-500 mr-2" /> **Conversations come first** — not endless headers, timestamps, and server metadata.
- <CheckCircle2 className="size-4 inline text-emerald-500 mr-2" /> **Replies feel continuous** — write your response right where you're reading.
- <EyeOff className="size-4 inline text-neutral-400 dark:text-neutral-500 mr-2" /> **Signal over noise** — signatures, legal disclaimers, and redundant quotes are collapsed automatically.
- <Sliders className="size-4 inline text-indigo-500 mr-2" /> **Contextual actions** — controls appear when you need them, not cluttering the canvas all the time.

We aren't trying to cram more buttons onto the screen. We're designing an interface that gets out of your way.

## 2. Speed Isn't Just About Loading Screens

A truly fast email application doesn't force you to wait after every action.

We designed Relay's client architecture around **TanStack Query** to deliver genuine sub-millisecond responsiveness:

- <Zap className="size-4.5 inline text-amber-500 mr-2" /> **Instant Optimistic Updates:** Mark an email as read or archive a thread, and the interface responds immediately without waiting for server acknowledgments.
- <RefreshCw className="size-4.5 inline text-blue-500 mr-2" /> **Silent Background Reconciliation:** State synchronizes seamlessly in the background and updates only what actually changed.
- <Inbox className="size-4.5 inline text-purple-500 mr-2" /> **Smart Prefetching:** Frequently accessed threads and upcoming conversations are pre-cached in memory so transitions are instant.

The philosophy is straightforward:

> **"The app should feel instant, even when the internet isn't."**

## 3. Meet Relay-Rover: On-Device Email Intelligence

Most email clients act as passive renderers — they receive raw HTML and leave the mental burden of parsing to you.

We built **Relay-Rover**, our client-side email extraction and classification engine that runs entirely on your device. Rover reads incoming message content, recognizes structural patterns, and surfaces the key data directly:

### What Rover Extracts and Surfaces Instantly:

- <KeyRound className="size-4.5 inline text-amber-500 mr-2" /> **Security & Auth:** OTPs, 2FA verification codes, and magic sign-in links with a single-tap **Copy** button.
- <Package className="size-4.5 inline text-blue-500 mr-2" /> **Shipping & Logistics:** Courier tracking codes with live delivery status.
- <CreditCard className="size-4.5 inline text-emerald-500 mr-2" /> **Finance & Commerce:** Transaction amounts, merchant receipts, and invoice references.
- <Plane className="size-4.5 inline text-sky-500 mr-2" /> **Travel & Itineraries:** Flight numbers, boarding times, gate updates, and hotel reservations.
- <Calendar className="size-4.5 inline text-purple-500 mr-2" /> **Schedules & Deadlines:** Event times, RSVPs, calendar invites, and due dates.

Instead of opening a 3-page email, scrolling through branding banners, and highlighting a six-digit code, Relay extracts the code and puts it right at your fingertips.

## 4. Purpose-Driven Categories (Beyond Generic Tabs)

An email isn't just "Promotions" or "Updates."

A bank transfer alert, an OTP confirmation, a flight itinerary, a coworker's query, and an online receipt all serve entirely distinct purposes.

Rover categorizes messages based on their true function using sender heuristics, structural markers, and content signatures.

### Why We Avoided Cloud AI APIs for Classification

Many modern apps simply forward every email to a remote LLM API. We intentionally rejected that approach:

- <Lock className="size-4.5 inline text-emerald-500 mr-2" /> **100% On-Device Privacy:** Your emails never leave your device to be parsed or classified.
- <Cpu className="size-4.5 inline text-indigo-500 mr-2" /> **Compiled Local Rules:** Pre-compiled regex patterns, keyword dictionaries, and structural matchers run natively.
- <Gauge className="size-4.5 inline text-amber-500 mr-2" /> **Zero Network Latency:** No roundtrips, no cloud API bills, and zero waiting for remote models.

> **"Build the intelligence once. Execute it across millions of emails locally in zero milliseconds."**

## 5. Not Every Email Deserves the Same UI

A two-sentence note from a friend and an elaborate marketing newsletter have nothing in common. Forcing them into the same template makes no sense.

Relay adapts its presentation dynamically based on what Rover detects:

- <MessageSquare className="size-4.5 inline text-emerald-500 mr-2" /> **Conversational Emails:** A clean, distraction-free reading canvas.
- <FileText className="size-4.5 inline text-blue-500 mr-2" /> **HTML Newsletters:** A structured, full-bleed document view preserving rich formatting.
- <Layers className="size-4.5 inline text-purple-500 mr-2" /> **Transactional Emails:** Clear summary cards with extracted details on top, with raw email preserved underneath.

The original email is always just a toggle away — nothing is ever lost or altered.
